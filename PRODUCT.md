# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kenyan and East African MSME owners, shopkeepers, and managers running daily trade from a phone on intermittent connectivity. They juggle sales, stock, invoices, expenses and cash flow between customers in Kiswahili and English, often with low digital literacy and high time pressure. Job-to-be-done: record a sale/order in <10s, see today's cash in one glance, chase an invoice via WhatsApp, and know what to restock — without leaving the shop floor.

## Product Purpose

Bizworth (FinAI/BizSawa) is the operating system for small trade: a mobile-first React Native + Expo app backed by Go, River and Postgres. It unifies POS (sales/orders), inventory, invoices + WAHA WhatsApp, expenses, payments (M-Pesa), and pre-computed analytics into one ink-first workspace. Success is a shop owner opening the app at 7am, confirming open orders, sending overdue invoices, and trusting the profit figure at night.

## Brand Personality

Calm confidence over hype. Ink + paper utility, not fintech neon. Three words: **trusted, grounded, precise**. Voice is direct, Kiswahili-ready, numbers-first. Feels like a well-kept ledger, not a dashboard toy.

## Anti-references

- SaaS cream/parchment 2026 default (warm-noise body bg), hero-metric cliché (big number + thin label + gradient), glassmorphism, side-stripe cards, border+soft-shadow ghost cards
- Identical icon+heading card grids, eyebrow kicker on every section, 32px+ card rounding, repeating-linear stripe backgrounds, gradient text, sketchy hand-drawn illustrations
- Dark-mode-by-default “tool looks cool” without ambient-light reason, saturated-beige brand wash

## Design Principles

1. **Show the money first** — revenue, profit, amount-due are always top, large, and contrast-safe (≥4.5:1).
2. **One thumb, one task** — every primary action is a 48px+ target, reachable at TAB_BAR_SCROLL_PADDING, with optimistic feedback.
3. **Paper logic, digital speed** — flows mirror a physical counter book (Sale → Order → Invoice → Payment) so mental models transfer.
4. **Explain the number** — every aggregate links to its bucket (category, date, customer segment) with trend + percentage.
5. **Quiet until needed** — restrained palette (ink, paper, one accent ≤10%), motion only to confirm (exp ease-out, no bounce), reduce-motion safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large ≥3:1, placeholder ≥4.5:1. Touch targets ≥44pt, labels + hitSlop, screen-reader names on tabs/FABs, dynamic scaling not clipped. Reduced-motion fallback crossfade. Bilingual (en-KE/sw) number/currency formatting KES, date en-KE.
